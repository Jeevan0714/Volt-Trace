## 💡 Quick Tips for Presenting VoltTrace

1. **Avoid Over-Technical Jargon:** Say *"digital fingerprint"* instead of *"hexadecimal SHA-256 digest"*. Say *"health check"* instead of *"electrochemical impedance spectroscopy"*.
2. **Emphasize the 82% Comparison (Slide 4):** This is the moment judges and listeners understand why VoltTrace exists. Spend deliberate time here!
3. **Stay Grounded in Ecology:** If asked *"Can this make recyclers rich?"*, answer: *"VoltTrace's goal is to make recyclers **safe, efficient, and ecologically responsible**. Financial savings in testing time are a natural byproduct of good engineering."*
